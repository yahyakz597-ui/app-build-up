10-DAY FOCUS CHALLENGE
The session grows a little every day, from 7 minutes on day 1 to 17 minutes on day 10.
How it works
  1. Cyclic sighing: two short inhales through the nose, then one long, slow exhale through the mouth. Follow the orb.
  2. Meditation: eyes closed, attention on a point behind your forehead or on your breath. When your mind wanders, gently come back. Every return is one rep.
  3. A bell marks the switch and the end. Finish the full session to count the day.

If you feel dizzy, breathe normally and take a break.
